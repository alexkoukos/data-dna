title: "Master services agreement: Delphi Insurance"
sensitivity: confidential

Master services agreement between Aegean Meridian Consulting, referred to as the supplier, and Delphi Insurance, referred to as the client. Confidential: for the contract team and the two account managers only.

Services. The supplier will provide data analytics and regulatory reporting services to the client, including the monthly solvency reports, the quarterly actuarial dashboards and ad hoc analysis requested by the client's finance department. The detailed scope of each project is set out in a statement of work signed by both parties.

Fees. The client will pay the supplier a fixed annual fee of eight hundred and forty thousand euros, invoiced quarterly in advance. Work outside the agreed scope is charged at the supplier's day rates, which are two hundred and sixty euros for an analyst and four hundred and ten euros for a senior consultant. Invoices are payable within thirty days. Late payment accrues interest at two percent above the European Central Bank rate.

Term. The agreement runs for three years from the first of January and renews automatically for periods of one year unless either party gives ninety days written notice before the end of the current period. After the first year the client may terminate for convenience by giving ninety days notice and paying fifty percent of the fees that would have been due for the rest of the current term.

Liability. The supplier's total liability under this agreement is limited to the fees paid by the client in the twelve months before the claim arose. This limit does not apply to breach of confidentiality, which is unlimited, nor to fraud or wilful misconduct. Neither party is liable for loss of profit or indirect damage.

Data protection. All personal data is processed inside the European Union. The supplier acts as a processor and will report any personal data breach to the client within twenty four hours of becoming aware of it. The supplier may not use a sub-processor without the written consent of the client.

Service levels. The reporting platform must be available ninety nine point five percent of the time, measured monthly and excluding planned maintenance announced five working days in advance. For every half percent below that target the client receives a credit of five percent of the quarterly fee, up to a maximum credit of twenty percent.

Confidentiality. Each party keeps the other's confidential information secret for the term of the agreement and for five years afterwards. Confidential information includes the client's policyholder data, the supplier's methodology and the terms of this agreement itself.

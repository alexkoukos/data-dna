Confidential. Salary bands 2027 of Aegean Meridian Consulting, only for the human resources department and management.

General principles. The bands are set every year by the human resources department together with management, based on the autumn market study and the company budget. Every position belongs to one band and every band has a minimum and a maximum annual salary. A person's salary cannot exceed the maximum of their band without management approval.

Developers. For developers the range of the first band is from twenty eight to thirty five thousand euros a year, of the second band from thirty five to forty five thousand and of the third band from forty five to sixty thousand euros. Tech leads range from sixty to eighty thousand euros and engineering directors from eighty to one hundred and twenty thousand euros.

Consultants. New consultants start at thirty two thousand euros. Senior consultants range from forty to sixty five thousand euros and project directors from sixty five to ninety thousand euros. The firm's partners have a separate scale set by management and not published.

Other departments. In the legal department the scale starts at thirty five thousand euros and reaches seventy thousand for senior lawyers. In accounting pay ranges from twenty six to fifty five thousand euros. In the human resources department pay ranges from twenty eight to fifty thousand euros.

Bonuses and raises. The performance bonus cannot exceed fifteen percent of base salary. Raises are approved once a year, in January, after review, and the average raise for 2027 must not exceed four percent of the payroll. Promotions are approved in the same period and cannot be combined with another raise.

Confidentiality. No information from this document may be shared with employees outside the human resources department, nor with external partners. Department heads are informed only about the scale of their own positions.

Confidential. Salary bands 2027 of Aegean Meridian Consulting, only for the human resources department and management.

General principles. The bands are set every year by the human resources department together with management, based on the autumn market study and the company budget. Every position belongs to one band and every band has a minimum and a maximum annual salary. A person's salary cannot exceed the maximum of their band without management approval.

Developers. For developers the range of the first band is from 28 to 35 thousand euros a year, of the second band from 35 to 45 thousand and of the third band from 45 to 60 thousand euros. Tech leads range from 60 to 80 thousand euros and engineering directors from 80 to 120 thousand euros.

Consultants. New consultants start at 32 thousand euros. Senior consultants range from 40 to 65 thousand euros and project directors from 65 to 90 thousand euros. The firm's partners have a separate scale set by management and not published.

Other departments. In the legal department the scale starts at 35 thousand euros and reaches 70 thousand for senior lawyers. In accounting pay ranges from 26 to 55 thousand euros. In the human resources department pay ranges from 28 to 50 thousand euros.

Bonuses and raises. The performance bonus cannot exceed 15 percent of base salary. Raises are approved once a year, in January, after review, and the average raise for 2027 must not exceed 4 percent of the payroll. Promotions are approved in the same period and cannot be combined with another raise.

Confidentiality. No information from this document may be shared with employees outside the human resources department, nor with external partners. Department heads are informed only about the scale of their own positions.

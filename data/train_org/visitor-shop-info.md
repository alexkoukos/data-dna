title: "Visitor and shop information"
sensitivity: public
---
Taygetos Oils welcomes visitors to the Kalamata mill from Monday to Saturday, with guided tastings at eleven in the morning and at four in the afternoon.

Orders from our online shop ship within two working days, and delivery is free in Greece for orders above sixty euros.

Unopened bottles can be returned within fourteen days of delivery for a full refund.

Our extra virgin olive oil is cold extracted within hours of harvest and comes in glass bottles of five hundred millilitres and one litre.

Schools and groups can book a free educational visit to the mill during the harvest season.

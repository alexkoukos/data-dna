Restricted. Taygetos Oils strategy for 2028, for the board and the executive team only.

We plan to grow revenue from forty-one million to fifty-six million euros in 2028, mainly through the German and Gulf markets.

Our gross margin target is forty-six percent, and we will raise list prices by six percent in March without telling distributors in advance.

The private label contract with Evripos Supermarkets is up for renewal and we are prepared to lose it rather than cut our price below the floor.

We will hire a Dubai sales lead on a package of ninety thousand euros and open a small warehouse there by September.

The main risk is the harvest: a poor season would cut our supply by a third and force us to buy oil from Spain at a higher cost.

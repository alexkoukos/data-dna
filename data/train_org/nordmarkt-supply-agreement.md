title: "Supply agreement: Nordmarkt Feinkost"
sensitivity: confidential
---
Confidential. Supply agreement between Taygetos Oils SA and Nordmarkt Feinkost GmbH, effective 1 January 2028.

Nordmarkt commits to buy at least one hundred and eighty thousand litres of extra virgin olive oil per year at a fixed price of seven euros and ten cents per litre.

Taygetos grants Nordmarkt exclusivity for retail distribution in Germany and Austria for three years, and may not supply any competing chain in those two countries.

If Taygetos delivers more than five working days late, Nordmarkt may deduct two percent of the invoice for each further week, up to ten percent of the order value.

Either party may terminate with one hundred and twenty days written notice, and the agreement renews automatically for two years unless notice is given.

Payment is due sixty days after delivery by bank transfer, and liability of Taygetos is capped at the fees paid in the previous eighteen months.

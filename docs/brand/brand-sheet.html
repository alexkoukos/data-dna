Thyro, the doorkeeper

Thyro is the gate itself. Named after the θυρωρός, the doorman every Athenian apartment block used to have: he knows who lives here and never raises his voice. The gate leaves show the decision (open, ajar, shut) and the lamp takes its color. The label always comes with it.

Usage

  • Clear space around the logo: the height of one pillar cap (the lamp's diameter) on every side, at least 1/4 of the mark height.
  • Minimum: mark 16 px (use the favicon drawing below 24 px), lockup 96 px wide.
  • Don't recolor the gate, stretch it, add effects, or place the dark logo on mid-tone photos.
